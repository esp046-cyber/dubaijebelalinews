Install this app for one-tap access and offline reading.

Headlines are aggregated from public feeds (Google News queries for Gulf News, Khaleej Times, The National and Dubai Media Office, plus Reddit r/dubai and r/uae). Tap a story to read it at the publisher. Metro status is timetable-based, so check RTA for live disruptions. Weather by Open-Meteo.

Community resources: agac.ae · Dubai Media Office

Add to Home Screen (iPhone / iPad)

  1. Open this page in Safari (not inside another app).
  2. Tap the Share button in the toolbar.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Keep the name DubaiNews and tap Add.

It then opens full-screen like a native app and works offline with your saved stories.
